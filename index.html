ABOUT THE ARTIST

Hello, I'm Anjali

Welcome to my art world. I am an artist who loves expressing creativity through colours, patterns and traditional Indian art.

My artwork is inspired by Madhubani painting, Indian culture, mythology and spiritual themes. I especially enjoy creating paintings of Maa Durga, Lord Ganesha, Lord Krishna and other traditional subjects.

Every painting is created with patience, imagination and love. Through my artwork, I want to share the beauty of Indian art and culture with everyone.
